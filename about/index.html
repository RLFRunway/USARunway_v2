Our Leadership Team

Gus Cardenas

Chief Executive Officer

Gus is a senior U.S. executive with more than three decades of leadership experience in strategic consulting, organizational development, and market expansion across Texas and the United States. His track record spans executive roles in business strategy, community and economic development, advising organizations on how to position themselves effectively in the U.S. market. His ability to navigate industry networks, interpret market signals, and guide organizational structuring makes him a strategic asset for international partners, including institutions in educational and international sectors.

Ron Farris

President

Ron Farris, Colonel, USAF, is a former NASA Johnson Space Center engineer and leader whose work shaped space shuttle rendezvous operations and the deployment and repair of the Hubble Space Telescope, earning the NASA Exceptional Achievement Medal. A command pilot with approximately 2,800 hours in the T-38, F-4, and F-16, he led USAF flight-training operations spanning six airbases, commanded USAF forces at Kandahar, and later directed operations at Morón Air Base, Spain and McMurdo Station, Antarctica. He holds B.S. and M.S. degrees in aerospace engineering from The University of Texas at Austin, which inducted him into its Aerospace Engineering Academy of Distinguished Alumni. At USARunway, Ron guides clients bringing advanced technologies to the U.S. for testing and deployment.

Monika Sebestova Sirilova

Chief Operating Officer

Monika oversees operational execution and U.S. readiness at USARunway. She combines legal, strategic, and technological expertise to support international organizations in establishing U.S. operations that are compliant, well-structured, and credible. She specializes in building the operational frameworks that U.S. institutions expect, including documentation readiness, internal processes, security and compliance alignment, and organizational preparedness. In client engagements, Monika ensures that program materials and operational elements align with U.S. standards, strengthening credibility and readiness when engaging with American institutions and partners.

Roman Dano

Senior Vice President, International Business Development

Roman is a business strategist, lobbyist, and investor whose network spans Slovakia, Poland, and broader Central Europe, with deep experience in cross-border market entry and business development. He has cultivated senior-level relationships with decision-makers and government-connected contacts across multiple sectors, operating at the intersection of business, politics, and investment where traditional business development rarely reaches. He is known for his ability to navigate both the political and commercial dimensions of complex market entry — understanding how decisions get made, who the real influencers are, and how to get the right people in the room.

Michael Skinta

Advisor, Defense & National Security

Mick Skinta is a retired U.S. Marine Corps Chief Warrant Officer 4 and “Gunner” — the Marine Corps' formal title for its weapons and infantry warrant officers — with two decades of frontline experience training Marine snipers and marksmen. He served as Regimental Gunner for the School of Infantry-West and held roles including Scout Sniper Instructor, Sniper Platoon Commander, and Company Gunnery Sergeant, earning the Legion of Merit. Since transitioning from active duty, Mick has worked in business development for major global defense and security companies, connecting defense technology providers with the operational Marine Corps and broader DoD community. Mick's rare combination of hands-on combat training leadership and defense-industry business development experience gives USARunway's clients a direct, credible perspective on what it takes for defense technology to earn trust and adoption within the U.S. military.

Phil M. Dolbow

Advisor, Defense & National Security

Phil Dolbow is a retired U.S. Army Sergeant Major with 40 years of experience in cybersecurity, secure communications, and defense-grade cloud systems. At Oracle, he serves as a cleared Master Cloud Consultant focused on security for classified and defense-sector cloud environments. He also serves as Chief Information Security Officer of SOFX.NET, a secure communications platform built by U.S. military veterans to Top Secret/SCI-grade standards. Phil's combination of military information-operations leadership, hands-on classified cloud security expertise, and deep familiarity with defense-grade compliance standards gives USARunway's clients direct insight into what it takes to build, secure, and operate in the U.S. defense and national security market.

Philip Sanger, MD

Advisor, Healthcare Innovation

Dr. Philip Sanger is Managing Partner of TEXO Ventures, an Austin-based healthcare venture capital firm he has led since 2009, and serves as USARunway's Advisor for Healthcare Innovation. A physician-turned-entrepreneur with more than 30 years in healthcare, Phil founded Intercede Health and helped launch HealthSpring, which went public in a $366M IPO before being acquired by Cigna for $3.8B. Through TEXO, he has backed and built a portfolio of health-tech companies including unicorn Lantern, CareDox, and Sensentia. His firsthand experience building, scaling, and exiting healthcare companies makes him a uniquely qualified guide for international health-tech founders navigating the U.S. market.
